# FACTS — Tây Du Ký: the pilgrimage as a staged production

Fact base for a 10-minute Vietnamese video essay. Claims about the novel are cited by chapter of the 100-chapter 世德堂 text (1592, attributed to 吳承恩 / Ngô Thừa Ân). Chinese quotations are from the public-domain text on Chinese Wikisource; Vietnamese renderings in the film are my own, never from a copyrighted translation.

## 1. The commission exists before the pilgrims (ch. 8)

- Buddha has the scriptures and decides to send them east; Guanyin is dispatched to find a seeker. [SAFE]
- Guanyin recruits each demon by offering a post: to Sha Wujing, 「你何不入我門來，皈依善果，跟那取經人做個徒弟，上西天拜佛求經？…那時節功成免罪，復你本職」 — "join, serve the scripture-seeker, and when the work is done your crime is cancelled and your old post restored." [SAFE, ch. 8]
- So the crew is cast, with contracts, before the journey starts; the "seekers" are staff with a career path.

## 2. The calamities are a numbered register (ch. 99)

- 觀音 keeps a 難簿 (register of calamities). The 五方揭諦 report it; the text lists them by number, ending 「…凌雲渡脫胎八十難。路經十萬八千里。聖僧歷難簿分明。」 [SAFE]
- 「菩薩將難簿目過了一遍，急傳聲道：『佛門中九九歸真。聖僧受過八十難，還少一難，不得完成此數。』即命揭諦：『趕上金剛，還生一難者。』」 — "The Bodhisattva looked through the register and said: in the Buddhist gate, nine nines return to truth; the holy monk has passed eighty, one short, the number cannot be completed. She ordered the 揭諦: catch up with the Vajras and make one more calamity." [SAFE — the central evidence]
- The list's first entry is 「金蟬遭貶第一難」 — the banishment from heaven, i.e. before the monk was born. Entries 2–3 are attempts on him as a baby (出胎幾殺, 滿月拋江). [SAFE] The count is cosmic, not a travel itinerary.
- Wukong knows the count: 「只因心裡明白，知道唐僧九九之數未完，還該有一難，故羈留於此。」 — he holds back at the river because he knows one calamity is still owed. [SAFE, ch. 99]

## 3. The demons are staff, and the novel says so (chs. 35, 39, 49, 71, 77)

- 金角/銀角: Laozi says 「那兩個怪：一個是我看金爐的童子，一個是我看銀爐的童子」 and then the key line: 「此乃海上菩薩問我借了三次，送他在此，託化妖魔，試你師徒可有真心往西去也。」 — "Guanyin borrowed them from me three times and sent them here, disguised as demons, to test whether you disciples truly mean to go west." [SAFE — the strongest single line for the thesis]
- Wukong's reaction: 「這菩薩也老大憊懶…如今反使精邪掯害。」 — "that Bodhisattva is a lazy piece of work… now she sends demons to squeeze us." [SAFE]
- 烏雞國: 文殊 arrives at the last moment: 「我來替你收這個妖怪的。」 The demon is his lion. [SAFE, ch. 39]
- 通天河: the demon is Guanyin's goldfish, raised in her lotus pond listening to sutras: 「他本是我蓮花池裡養大的金魚，每日浮頭聽經，修成手段。」 [SAFE, ch. 49]
- 朱紫國: the demon is her 金毛犼, which "got loose while the shepherd slept" — 「因牧童盹睡，失於防守，這孽畜咬斷鐵索走來」 — and she adds that it was there to 「與朱紫國王消災」, to cancel the king's debt. [SAFE, ch. 71]
- 獅駝嶺: lion and elephant are Manjusri's and Samantabhadra's mounts; the roc (大鵬) is the Buddha's "uncle" by the peacock story: 「孔雀…我在雪山頂上…被他吸下肚去…故此留他在靈山會上，封他做佛母孔雀大明王菩薩。大鵬是與他一母所生，故此有些親處。」 [SAFE, ch. 77]
- The roc refuses heaven on material grounds: 「你那裡持齋把素，極貧極苦；我這裡吃人肉，受用無窮。」 [SAFE]
- Demons with no owner are killed and processed: the three rhinos of 青龍山 (ch. 92) are killed, their horns sawn off, one horn sent to 靈山, the meat shared out. [SAFE]
- Modern summary, NOT a novel quote: 「有背景的妖怪都被收走了，沒背景的都被打死了」. Treat as a contemporary reading; no source in the Ming text. [HEDGE / label as "câu này không có trong truyện"]

## 4. The road is not transport (ch. 22)

- Wukong's somersault covers 108,000 li: 「老孫的觔斗雲，一縱有十萬八千里。」 [SAFE]
- 八戒 asks why he does not simply carry the master over; the answer is that a mortal body is heavy as a mountain and cannot be carried by cloud. [SAFE, ch. 22] The novel never lets the journey be shortened: the sutras are given to the one who walked.

## 5. The product is a transaction (chs. 12, 98, 100)

- The pilgrimage is a state project: the Tang emperor builds the 水陸大會 (1,200 monks, 49 days) to settle the ghosts of his dream, and sends the monk as his representative. [SAFE, chs. 11–12]
- At the send-off the emperor asks when he will return; 三藏: 「只在三年，徑回上國。」 — "three years, and I am back." [SAFE, ch. 12]
- Actual duration: 14 years / 5,048 days; the sutras: 5,048 scrolls, 「乃一藏之數」. [SAFE, ch. 100] 5,048 is a canonical number, not a coincidence.
- First handing: blank scrolls. 「白本者，乃無字真經，倒也是好的。」 [SAFE, ch. 98]
- The price: Ananda and Kasyapa ask for 人事 (a gift). Buddha's ruling: 「經不可輕傳，亦不可以空取。」 He cites a previous sale for 「三斗三升米粒黃金」 and says they sold too cheap. [SAFE, ch. 98]
- The monk gives the 紫金缽盂, the emperor's own gift. 「那阿儺接了，但微微而笑。」 The other attendants mock him — 「不羞，不羞，需索取經的人事」 — and he still will not let go of the bowl. [SAFE]
- Only then are the written scrolls handed over: 5,048. [SAFE]

## 6. The one thing not in the script (ch. 99)

- The 81st calamity is the turtle dumping them in the river because the monk forgot to ask its lifespan. The packages are soaked. [SAFE]
- The 佛本行經 is torn on the drying stone: 「所以至今《佛本行經》不全」. [SAFE]
- Wukong: 「蓋天地不全，這經原是全全的，今沾破了，乃是應不全之奧妙也，豈人力所能與耶？」 — "heaven and earth are incomplete; the sutras were whole; the tearing answers the mystery of incompleteness; no human power could help it." [SAFE — the film's final idea]

## 7. The ford and the ending (chs. 98, 100)

- At 凌雲渡 the pilgrims cross on a bottomless boat; a corpse floats downstream and all of them say: 「那個原來是你。」 — "that was you." [SAFE, ch. 98]
- Titles: 唐僧 = 旃檀功德佛, 悟空 = 鬥戰勝佛. [SAFE, ch. 100]
- The band is gone: 「今已成佛，自然去矣」. Wukong asks for it to be smashed 「切莫叫那甚麼菩薩再去捉弄他人」 — "so that Bodhisattva never plays that trick on anyone else." [SAFE, ch. 100]

## 8. The counter-reading (must be in the film)

- 胡適, 《西遊記考證》(1923): the novel's value is in its 「尖刻的玩世主義」 (a biting world-mocking humour); he attacks the allegorical readers who 「妄想透過紙背去尋那『微言大義』」 — who want a hidden doctrine behind the page. [SAFE, source above]
- The Ming 心猿 reading: the monkey is the mind, the journey is cultivation; the demons are the self. This is a real tradition (commentaries from the Ming–Qing), and it is the strongest objection to "it is only a play". [SAFE as a tradition; no single quote used]
- Therefore the film's claim is limited and defensible: the novel *builds* the pilgrimage as a managed production (the text says so at ch. 35 and ch. 99), and that production is the point — the ritual, not the scrolls.
