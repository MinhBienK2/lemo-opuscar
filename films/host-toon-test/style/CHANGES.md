# Talking-Head Host Toon — decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base, 80s Cel Anime (cel-anime-80s), or "—" for something new.

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Essence | 80s OVA: painted backgrounds, backlit cels, CRT playback | Modern clean cel; one recurring host in four framings (desk, pop-in, in the scene, reaction close-up) with b-roll in the same line language | A host for niche documentary / explainer channels; the user wants her in the story too ("Zack D Films, but 2D") |
| 2 Materials | Cel + airbrushed backgrounds + tape/CRT pass | Cel only, flat paper ground with grain; no airbrush, no CRT | Clean, readable at YouTube sizes; looks current |
| 2 Host | — | Nhi: young scholar, bob + ahoge, round gold glasses, pencil pin, amber eyes; 2.5D head, finger-table hands, IK limbs; costumes scholar / heist / field | Cute, scholarly, young; must act and gesture in any situation |
| 3 Colour | Neon night palettes | Cream ground, ink `#2a1f2a`, teal host, highlighter yellow `#ffd23f` accent, one story colour per film | A recognisable channel look; yellow = "this matters" |
| 4 Type | Period title cards | Be Vietnam Pro (titles, subtitles) + Patrick Hand (card labels) | Full Vietnamese diacritics; friendly |
| 5 Motion | On twos/threes, held drawings | Rig on ones, mouths at 12 fps from the voice, back-ease pops | Talking heads need smooth, lively acting |
| 6 Camera | Pans over painted BGs, speed lines | Desk push-ins, host→card pans, punch-in reactions, whip pans, costume step-in wipes | Explainer rhythm |
| 7 Sound | City-pop, tape hiss | One narrator voice (HoaiMy), light pizzicato/marimba bed, UI-style foley (pop, paper, stamp, marker, ting) | Voice-led documentary |
| 8 Native moves | Backlit glow, tape glitches | Glasses glint, highlighter swipe, pop-in host, costume step-in, reaction close-up, plan-vs-reality card | The channel's signature beats |
