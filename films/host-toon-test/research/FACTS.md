## 1. Premise

- The Professor [Giáo sư / "El Profesor"], real name Sergio Marquina, mastermind of the Royal Mint of Spain heist. Alias to the police: Salvador "Salva" Martin. [W1, W2: "The Professor (Sergio Marquina) ... is the mastermind of the heist who assembled the group"]
- Origin of the plan: the idea was his father's. Jesus Marquina robbed banks to pay for young Sergio's hospital treatment and dreamed of robbing a mint; he was shot dead by police during a bank robbery on 5 Feb 1987 while Sergio watched. [FC The_Professor] Wikipedia: "The idea of the first heist is to avenge his father, whose idea it originally was." [W2] In ep 12 (P1) the Professor tells Nairobi he got the inspiration "to print money without robbing or hurting anyone" in memory of his father. [FE(1,12)]
- Recruitment: Ep 1 opens with the Professor saving Tokyo from police after a failed robbery and proposing a "heist of massive proportions". [W1] Wikipedia: he recruits "a group of eight people, who choose city names as their aliases for anonymity". [W1] Fandom ep 1: a stranger "proposes that she join his heist - where he plans to print EUR 2.4 billion"; she then finds herself with seven other robbers in a house in Toledo. [FE(1,1)] How he found/vetted the other seven: UNVERIFIED (episodes show it in flashback; no source I could open states the selection criteria; one aggregator says "nothing to lose", not reliable).
- Rules [Quy tắc]:
  - Spanish press wording: "nada de nombres, sin preguntas personales, prohibidas las relaciones amorosas" and "la mas inquebrantable de todas: no debe haber ni una gota de sangre" [LN]. So: no real names (hence city code names), no personal questions, no relationships, and no blood.
  - Fandom abbreviates it NPPRP: "Ningun Nombre, Preguntas Personales, o Relaciones Personales"; goal "without shedding the single drop of blood in order to remain 'Robin Hoods'". [FH]
  - CONFLICT: Wikipedia's plot says the robbers "are warned that there will be casualties". [W1] Fandom and Spanish press say "no blood" is the rule, and Ep 4 (P1) has Berlin saying "the rule of 'no blood' has been broken" after Denver fakes a hostage's execution. [FE(1,4)] I trust "no blood / nobody should die" as the stated rule (two sources + episode behaviour); the Wikipedia line is probably a loose paraphrase. For the film: "rule: nobody gets hurt".
  - Rules were broken almost at once: Rio gives Tokyo his dog-tag with his real name the day before the heist (ep 1) and the two become lovers. [FE(1,1)]
- Training: five months at an abandoned hunting estate in the Toledo countryside. [W1: "five months of preparation at an abandoned hunting estate in the Toledo countryside"; WES "durante cinco meses"; LN "cinco meses"]
- Cover: the Professor's alias Salva claims to be starting a cider business on his grandfather's orchard. [FC The_Professor trivia]

How the three die:
- Oslo: hit on the head with an iron rod by a hostage (Jacinto) during the hostage breakout, Ep 12 (P1); left in a vegetative state; in Ep 1 (P2) Helsinki suffocates him with a pillow as a mercy, at Oslo's earlier request. [FC Oslo; FE(1,12); FE(2,1)] Important: not killed by the police.
- Moscow: hit by police fire as Tokyo rides back into the Mint, Ep 6 (P2); "three bullets in his stomach"; dies in Ep 8 (P2), with Denver at his side. [FE(2,6), FE(2,7), FE(2,8)]. Wikipedia only says "Oslo, Moscow and Berlin are killed". [W1]
- Berlin: stays behind in the vault to hold off the police so the others can leave, taking the bullets standing up; "shot multiple times by a S.W.A.T team". [FE(2,9); W3: "Berlin sacrifices himself so that the gang can escape, dying under police fire"]. His hostage-lover Ariadna is rescued.
- Berlin is the Professor's older brother: Wikipedia: the Professor is "Berlin's long lost brother" [W2]; fandom clarifies half-brothers (different paternal surnames; mother's side implied) [FC Berlin: "The Professor (younger half-brother)"]. Correct to use in the film. Production note: the link "was not in the original script, but was built into the characters' backstory at the end of part 1" after the actors proposed it [W1]. For viewers it lands late; Berlin and the Professor's relationship is stated in the final episodes.
- Later deaths (Nairobi, Tokyo) are after Part 2 and must not appear.

## 3. The plan

- Why a mint, not a bank vault: a bank only holds existing money (and it is marked, counted, traceable). The Mint "is where money is printed"; the crew does not take existing cash, "they print new money themselves". [LP] Fandom: "to print their own untraceable banknotes without shedding a single drop of blood". [FH] Press: robbers aim to leave with "dinero recien impreso y sin registros" (freshly printed money with no records). [LN] The Professor's pitch (framing, not stealing from anybody): fandom summary of Ep 8 (P2) has him explaining "how the European Central Bank produces hundreds of billions of euros every year only to line the pockets of the rich, yet no one calls them thieves"; the exact line is UNVERIFIED (fandom paraphrase; I could not open a transcript). Ep 12 (P1): "print money without robbing or hurting anyone". [FE(2,8), FE(1,12)]
- Target vs actual (see section 7): plan = EUR 2.4 billion in EUR 50 notes within 11 days [FH: "print EUR 2.4 billion in small denominations of 50 euros, in less than eleven days"]; actual = EUR 984 million after 128 hours. 2.4 billion / 50 = 48 million notes [FA says "48 millones de billetes", ~44.2 tons: derived arithmetic matches].
- Days: planned 11 days [W1 "plans to remain inside for 11 days"; WES "11 dias de reclusion"; LN "11 largos dias"]. Actual: "after 128 hours" [W1]. FH timeline: entry Fri 21 Oct 2016 ~10 a.m. (10:16), end Wed 26 Oct 2016 18:32. Computed: 5 days 8 h 16 min = 128 h 16 min, consistent with 128 h. 128 h = 5.33 days = 48.5% of the 11 planned days. (Exact clock times are fandom; UNVERIFIED elsewhere.)
- Hostages: 67 [W1 "take 67 hostages"; WES "67 rehenes"; FA; LN "Son 67"; FH]. Includes the Mint staff and a school group (Brighton College). 16-17 hostages escape in Ep 12 (P1): fandom says Pablo "had escaped along with 15 other hostages" [FE(1,13)]; Wikipedia's episode list paraphrase says seventeen (Ep 9 original); exact number UNVERIFIED (16 vs 17).
- Red jumpsuits + Dali masks: robbers wear them; in Ep 2 (P1) "All hostages are given a red jumpsuit and a fake gun" [FE(1,2)], and Alison is forced to announce on video that all hostages are dressed identically [FH: "This makes police hesitant about entering the mint"]. Mónica announces by megaphone "that the hostages are dressed exactly the same of the robbers, so any attempts of assault could cost lives" [FH]. On the roof in Ep 4 (P1) "Everyone is wearing a red jumpsuit and a Dali mask, so that the police cannot identify whether they are hostages or robbers" and police shoot hostage Arturo by mistake. [FE(1,4)] Wikipedia: "Dressed in red jumpsuits and masks depicting artist Salvador Dali" [W1]. In the finale the hostages are told to put masks on again so the police can't tell them from robbers. [FE(2,9)]
- The Alison Parker angle: daughter of the British ambassador, nicknamed "Little Lamb"; on the Brighton College school visit the day the Professor picks (21 Oct). "They planned on using her to give them more bargaining power ... This also pressured the police to try to forcibly enter." [FH] In Ep 1 fandom: she is "the daughter of the British Ambassador, who is a close friend of the Queen of England" [FE(1,1)]; Prieto says rescuing her is a priority "to prevent diplomatic issues" [FE(1,2)]. The Professor lets police enter early (as predicted); police see the masked armed robbers through an endoscope camera and retreat. [FE(1,2)]
- Tunnel/exit: the intended exit is a pre-existing tunnel from a Mint vault to the Professor's hangar, which Moscow digs into; the tunnel the hostages dig (up to "48 metres underground before they get to a sewer pipe") is a red herring. [FM: "the robbers force some hostages to dig a tunnel as a red herring, while Moscow digs into an already built tunnel from one of the vaults, connecting to the professor's hangar. This tunnel is the robber's intended escape route."] Wikipedia: they plan to "print and escape ... through a self-built escape tunnel". [W1] Fandom Ep 6 (P2): four Serbs who "dug a tunnel five years before" stand by. [FE(2,6)] (Conflict: "self-built" [W1] vs "already built" [FM]; for the film say "a tunnel to the Professor's hangar".)
- Named contingencies [FH, FE]:
  - Plan Valencia: fake massacre noise ("robbers shoot rollers, hostages scream") to provoke police into a hasty reaction; used Ep 9 (P1). [FH; FE(1,9)]
  - Plan Cameroon: win the public as the underdog (like Cameroon vs Brazil); used Ep 4 (P2). [FH; FE(2,4)]
  - Trojan Horse: police send undercover Angel in as a nurse; Professor bugs his glasses (Ep 5 P1). [FE(1,5); FH]
  - Plan Chernobyl: a last-resort plan told only to Berlin: "release EUR 1 billion from the sky over Madrid, and then disappearing altogether amid the resulting chaos" [FE(2,4)]; fandom: "create a commotion in the streets. This plan did not end up being used." [FH] Berlin refuses to trigger it because "all the money would be lost" (Ep 1 P2). [FE(2,1)] A Part 3 page notes the money-from-a-zeppelin scene "was Plan Chernobyl of the first heist" [FC Aikido]. Tokyo tortures Berlin for it in Ep 2 (P2). [FE(2,2)]
  - Golden rule: "buy time". [FE(2,2)]
- The Professor's hangar: from a hangar "nearby" he talks to the robbers by cable/phone [FE(1,1)]; "Hangar HQ" is the Professor's base; police are given the address only at the end (Raquel gives Alcantara 33 under pressure; the police find it empty). [FE(2,9)] Whether the hangar equals the "warehouse" cider front Raquel visits (Ep 12 P1): UNVERIFIED.
- The Professor and Raquel Murillo: she is Inspector (divorced, daughter Paula, mother Mari with Alzheimer's), the police's negotiator. Ex-husband Alberto beat her for years. [FE(1,2), FE(1,4)] They first meet at Bar Hanoi (Cafe Hanoi) near the Mint at ~06:25 on Saturday, 20 hours into the heist, when she quits by phone and her phone dies and "a stranger" lends his; the Professor deliberately keeps coming back to this bar. [FH; FE(1,3)] He plays "Salva". She sleeps with him (Ep 12), then learns the truth via Angel's messages (Ep 13 P1), later his orange clown wig (Ep 5 P2) and polygraph confession (Ep 6 P2). He almost poisons her mother, Mari, with dioxin in Ep 13 but knocks the cup away. [FE(1,13)] By Ep 8 (P2) Raquel is off the case with an arrest warrant out for her, hunts him herself, finds the Professor and kisses him. [FE(2,8)]
- The bar: Bar Hanoi (Cafe Hanoi) [FH, FE(1,3), FE(1,4), FE(2,5)]. In Ep 5 (P2) the Professor shows Raquel beach photos and randomly picks Palawan, Philippines. [FE(2,5)]

## 4. Beat outline (Netflix numbering)

1. Tokyo is on the run, a failed robbery; the Professor rescues and recruits her, "a heist of massive proportions". [W1; FE(1,1)]
2. Five months in a Toledo estate: eight criminals get city names and four rules (no names, no personal questions, no relationships, no blood). [LN; W1]
3. Fri 21 Oct 2016: they hijack the paper delivery convoy, take police uniforms, walk into the Mint in red jumpsuits and Dali masks, fake a robbery ("pretend to come out, shoot in the air and re-enter") and lock in with 67 hostages. [FE(1,1); FH] Turn: the "robbery" is a lie; the point is to buy time.
4. Raquel Murillo is assigned as negotiator; Colonel Prieto (intelligence) overrides her. [FE(1,1-2)]
5. Alison Parker, the ambassador's daughter, is discovered among the hostages; police try to enter early, as the Professor predicted, and see masked machine-gunners and retreat. [FE(1,2)] Turn: the hostages are dressed as robbers, so police can't assault.
6. Printing starts: staff print EUR 50s; "EUR 52 million" printed by morning. [FE(1,2-3)]
7. Sat ~06:25: Raquel meets a stranger at Bar Hanoi who lends her a phone: the Professor. [FH; FE(1,3)]
8. Alison's phone photo leaks Rio's face; police ID Tokyo and Rio. Berlin has Rio beaten. [FE(1,3)]
9. Mónica's hidden phone rings; Berlin orders Denver to kill her; Denver shoots her thigh and hides her instead. The "no blood" rule is dented. [FE(1,3-4)]
10. Police shoot hostage Arturo on the roof by mistake (Dali mask confusion). [FE(1,4)]
11. Trojan Horse: Angel enters as a "nurse"; the Professor bugs his glasses and reads the police plan. [FE(1,5)]
12. The 1992 Seat Ibiza clue; the Professor sneaks into a junkyard; Berlin's jacket button planted; Raquel's recording ("save Alison over eight hostages") leaks, public opinion flips toward the robbers. [FE(1,7-8)]
13. Plan Valencia: fake massacre; Berlin's illness revealed (Helmer's myopathy, months to live). [FE(1,9-10)]
14. Raquel and "Salva" fall for each other; Angel suspects, crashes his car (comatose). [FE(1,11-12)]
15. Hostage breakout led by Jacinto; Oslo is clubbed into a coma; 16-17 hostages flee. [FE(1,12-13)]
16. Raquel learns Salva is the Professor's mole; the Professor nearly poisons her mother and stops. [FE(1,13)]
17. P2: Police comb the Toledo house, find staged DNA; Professor arrested then freed by Raquel; Helsinki ends Oslo's suffering; Berlin refuses Plan Chernobyl; Tokyo mutinies and is turned over to police. [FE(2,1-3)]
18. Nairobi takes charge ("the matriarchy begins" [W1]); Plan Cameroon: win public via press. [FE(2,4)]
19. Raquel spots the clown wig and the Professor is exposed; she ties him up in Toledo, he confesses he's Sergio Marquina. [FE(2,5-6)]
20. Four Serbs free Tokyo; she rides back into the Mint; Moscow is shot. [FE(2,6)]
21. Denver and Rio break through the tunnel; Moscow dies; coffins of Moscow and Oslo; Nairobi shows "EUR 984 million, much less than their target of EUR 2.4 billion". [FE(2,8)]
22. Raquel, off the case, finds the Professor at his warehouse; he explains money, ECB; she kisses him and leaves for the hospital. [FE(2,8)]
23. Police storm the Mint; masked hostages file out; Berlin stays behind in the vault with Ariadna; the Professor begs him to leave; Berlin dies standing. [FE(2,9)]
24. The money leaves by tunnel, in drums onto trucks; the Professor and Helsinki drive off; the others change clothes and leave one by one; Denver and Mónica walk out; police find the hangar empty. [FE(2,8-9)]
25. A year later: Raquel in Palawan; the Professor offers his phone charger. [FE(2,9); W1]

## 5. Ending of Part 2

- Escapers: the Professor, Tokyo, Nairobi, Rio, Denver, Helsinki and Stockholm (Mónica). Dead: Oslo, Moscow, Berlin. [W1; FE(2,9)]
- Money: leaves through the tunnel to the hangar, loaded in drums onto trucks; crew leaves disguised and separately. Press-level check: "At the end of part 2, after 128 hours, the robbers escape from the Mint with EUR 984 million" [W1].
- Epilogue: Raquel arrested, gives the police a decoy address ("Alcantara 33") under pressure [FE(2,9)]; she chooses not to hand the Professor over ("emotionally unable to hand him over" [W1]). One year later: "Raquel finds a series of postcards left by the Professor, who wrote the coordinates for a location in Palawan in the Philippines, where she reunites with him". [W1] On screen: she sits in a Palawan beach bar, her phone dies; "The Professor offers his [charger]". [FE(2,9)]. How/when she gets out of custody is not shown: UNVERIFIED.

## 6. Hook facts (with sources)

1. They didn't steal: they printed. The Mint is "where money is printed", so the crew prints "brand new money" instead of taking any. [LP] Possible hook line (VI): "Họ không cướp tiền của ai cả, vì tiền do chính họ in ra." Plan and justification: [FH, FE(2,8)].
2. They aimed for EUR 2.4 billion in 11 days but walked out with EUR 984 million after 128 hours (about 41% of the target, in about 48% of the planned time). [FE(2,8): "printed EUR 984 million, much less than their target of EUR 2.4 billion"; W1: "128 hours ... EUR 984 million"; FH]. Derived: 984/2400 = 41%; 128/264 = 48.5%; 984M/128h = ~7.7 M per hour (fandom says printing started at "EUR 8 million per hour" [FH]).
3. The heist was his father's dream: Sergio watched his bank-robber father shot dead in 1987; the Mint was the one thing his father never finished. [FC The_Professor; W2]
4. Real-world: the Mint on screen is actually the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) building; the sets were destroyed after Part 2; the robbers' banknotes were "printed with permission of the Bank of Spain and had an increased size as an anti-counterfeit measure". [W1 "Filming", "Design"; FM]
5. The show was a TV flop turned Netflix hit: 15 episodes on Antena 3 (2017), re-cut into 22 by Netflix, became the most-watched non-English series on Netflix in early 2018; "Bella ciao", an Italian anti-fascist song, became a European summer hit in 2018. [W1] Bonus: the Professor's grandfather "had fought against the fascists in Italy". [W2]
6. (Reserve) The Dali mask choice drew criticism from the Gala-Salvador Dali Foundation for not requesting permissions. [W1] The Mint's real counterpart prints a lot: Merca2's headline says the FNMT "participara en la impresion de mas de 200.000 M EUR en 2020", so the heist's 2.4 billion is about 1.2% of one real year (derived; headline only, UNVERIFIED in body). [M2]
